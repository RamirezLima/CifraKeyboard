Cifra Keyboard encrypts your messages inside the keyboard, before any app receives them. Chat and e-mail apps only ever see encrypted text, so your privacy does not depend on their encryption.

<b>Cifra Keyboard is a fork of Simple Keyboard</b> by Raimondas Rimkus and contributors, which is based on the AOSP LatinIME keyboard. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Simple Keyboard authors.

<b>Warning:</b> Cifra Keyboard is in development and has not been independently audited.

<b>How it works:</b>

* Create an identity from 12 or 24 BIP39 words (English list) and an optional passphrase. The words are never stored.
* Add your contacts' public keys and compare their fingerprints.
* Tap the lock, pick a contact and type. The text stays inside the keyboard until you tap Encrypt and approve the result.
* Copy an encrypted message you received and tap Read: the text is shown only inside the keyboard.

<b>Security:</b>

* Optional cold phone: keys stay on a second phone that never goes online, and encrypted messages travel by QR code
* No internet permission, no backups
* Private keys sealed by the Android Keystore (StrongBox when available)
* Screenshots and screen recording blocked on the keyboard
* HPKE (RFC 9180) with X25519 and ChaCha20-Poly1305

<b>Inherited from Simple Keyboard:</b>

* Adjustable keyboard height
* Number row
* Swipe space to move the cursor, delete swipe
* Custom theme colors
